Visitor sheet dates: the brief’s own spellings (2/12/2024, 15.12.24, 2-12-24, Bangla digits, ISO, a real xlsx cell) are all read day-first with two-digit years as 20xx, an ambiguous day/month pair is named as ambiguous rather than quietly chosen, and a month-first row is read only when it is the only possible reading and reports the exception; impossible dates (31/02, 31/11, day 0) and unreadable ones are refused with the owner’s own text and a sentence in both languages, and a four-digit year outside 2000–2100 is not told it was read as 20xx; a future or five-year-old date is flagged on its row; and a year that disagrees with the rest of the sheet is flagged with the sheet’s own year suggested — never applied, never suggested into the future, and never flagged when there is no majority.
